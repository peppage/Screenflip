Features

Built for one job, done well

Instant primary swap

Makes your OLED the primary display with one click, using native Windows display APIs. Fullscreen games and overlays land where you want them.

Dim the other screen

Covers your secondary monitor with a near-black overlay so it won't distract you or wash out your OLED's contrast while you play.

Flip on, flip off

Enable to enter game mode; enable again to put everything back exactly as it was. Same window, same button. Just a toggle.

Lives in the tray

Minimizes to the notification area and stays out of your way. The tray icon shows at a glance whether game mode is currently active.

Kind to your OLED

Built for setups where the OLED sits secondary to avoid burn-in day to day, but needs to be primary for fullscreen gaming.

Tiny and portable

A single executable! No installer, no background services, under 150 MB. Built with C# and native Win32 calls. Nothing extra to add.

How it works

Three clicks, start to finish

1

Open ScreenFlip and hit Enable

Your OLED becomes the primary display and the other screen dims. From here every fullscreen game opens on the OLED.

2

Play on your OLED

Games, launchers, and overlays all land on the right display. The dimmed second monitor stays quietly out of view.

3

Hit Enable again to flip back

Done for the night? One more click restores your primary monitor and clears the overlay. Everything's back to normal.
